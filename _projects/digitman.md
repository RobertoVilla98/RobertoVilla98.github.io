The **DIGITMAN** project (*Occupant-based digital predictive management to improve the built environment*) is a research initiative funded by the Italian PRIN 2022 program under the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR). It is a collaboration between Università Politecnica delle Marche (UNIVPM - coordinator), Alma Mater Studiorum - Università di Bologna (UNIBO), and Politecnico di Milano (POLIMI).

### Project Overview
This research addresses the lack of digitalization in public building stocks by developing a common digital framework and logbook. The platform integrates dynamic occupancy data, which is typically omitted in traditional facility management, with building performance metrics.

The project focuses on three areas:
- **Operation**: Optimizing building energy consumption and indoor comfort (IEQ) based on actual occupancy profiles.
- **Maintenance**: Predictive modeling and automatic assignment of maintenance priorities using machine learning (Bi-LSTM and decision tree algorithms) applied to unstructured request logs.
- **Safety**: Code-based verification of width, length, and redundancy of escape routes (fire safety) based on occupant load distributions.

For more details, visit the official [DIGITMAN Website](https://www.digitman-project.net/).
