The **OFFICIO** project is an ongoing research initiative focused on office workspace optimization, developing data-driven methods to improve occupant well-being and energy performance.

### Project Overview
The research investigates:
- **Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ)**: Multidimensional assessment of thermal, acoustic, visual, and indoor air quality conditions in office settings.
- **Occupant Behavior & Space Utilization**: Tracking spatial utilization and occupant interaction with building systems to improve operations.
- **Building Automation & Controls**: Designing smart, occupant-centric controls for heating, cooling, lighting, and ventilation to reduce carbon footprint while supporting productivity and safety.

For more details, visit the official [OFFICIO Project Website](https://progetto-officio.it/).
