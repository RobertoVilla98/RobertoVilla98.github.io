This research, conducted as part of the ENEA-funded "Ricerca di Sistema Elettrico" program, developed and validated lightweight building envelopes to improve building physics performance.

### Project Overview
The project focused on the design, simulation, and experimental testing of light-gauge steel and timber dry-assembly building envelopes to achieve high thermal performance and structural resilience:
- **Energy Performance**: High thermal resistance, dynamic heat transfer optimization, and integration with renewable energy sources.
- **Seismic Resilience**: Structural behavior of lightweight components under earthquake conditions.
- **Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ)**: Acoustic, thermal, and hygrothermal comfort validation.

For more details, visit the [BEE Lab Research Page](https://www.beelab.polimi.it/research/).
