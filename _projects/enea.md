Collaborated on the completed research project focused on **innovative dry-assembly building envelopes** for sustainable and seismic-resilient structures, as part of the "Ricerca di Sistema Elettrico" program funded by **ENEA** (Italian National Agency for New Technologies, Energy and Sustainable Economic Development).

### Project Overview
The research aimed to develop, simulate, and experimentally validate new lightweight building envelopes (steel-frame and timber-frame dry systems) to optimize building physics properties, including:
- **Energy Performance**: High thermal resistance, dynamic heat transfer optimization, and integration with renewable energies.
- **Seismic Resilience**: Structural behavior of lightweight components under earthquake conditions.
- **Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ)**: Acoustic, thermal, and hygrothermal comfort validation.

For more details, visit the [BEE Lab Research Page](https://www.beelab.polimi.it/research/).
