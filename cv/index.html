Professional Summary

Roberto Villa, Building and Architectural Engineer, is a PhD Candidate (41st cycle) in Architectural Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, Department of Architecture, Built Environment and Construction Engineering (DABC), and a licensed Professional Civil and Environmental Engineer. He holds a single-cycle Master’s Degree in Building Engineering - Architecture from Politecnico di Milano. His research within the Building performancE façadE Laboratory (BEE Lab) focuses on AECO digitalization, Building Information Modeling (BIM), Building Performance Simulation (BPS), and IoT-enabled semantic Digital Twins for predictive building management. Villa actively contributes to key national research initiatives, including the MUR PRIN DIGITMAN project and the ENEA triennial research agreement on building decarbonization. He serves on the organizing and steering committees for the Dot.AT.i Study Day conference and is involved in co-supervising Master’s theses and drafting research proposals. Alongside his engineering background, he utilizes advanced computational workflows in Python, Machine Learning, and data mining to optimize building performance telemetry.
